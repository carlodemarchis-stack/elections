How it is scored. The forecast gives each race a chance for each party; where an independent can win, they get a share too. A race counts as called the right way if the party with the highest chance won. The Brier score measures the percentages themselves: for each race it adds up the squared gap between the forecast chance and what happened (1 for the winner, 0 for the others), then averages. Zero is perfect, and a 50/50 guess between two parties scores 0.5. A good forecast is also calibrated: of the races it gave a 90% chance, about nine in ten should go that way.

The forecast is civicAPI's, saved by us; the calls come from civicAPI too. The chance a forecast gives each party is treated as a chance of winning, which fits its numbers but has not been confirmed with civicAPI.
