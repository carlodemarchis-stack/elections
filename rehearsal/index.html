What the rehearsal has taught us so far

  • Seats are a running total. civicAPI's national "seats" figure is the seats not up plus the races called so far, not a fixed number. We had treated it as fixed, which would have double-counted the Senate bar as soon as races were called. Fixed; the totals now match civicAPI's own (House 229–206, Senate 51–49, governors 25–25 at the end of the 27 October test).
  • Test data must never look real. The feed flags it (test_data). Outside rehearsal mode the pages ignore it and say so, and the archive keeps it apart from the real results.
  • A forecast can be published broken. The 27 October Senate forecast had seat totals of 135 in a 100-seat chamber and a 100% chance of control for both parties. The pages now fall back to the last sane forecast and say why.

Before the night

On the morning of 3 November, check the flag at the top of this page. It must say the test election is off. If civicAPI leaves it on, the live page would treat the real results as test data and ignore them, so the first thing to do on the day is look here.
